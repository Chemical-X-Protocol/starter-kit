### Fixed (#7531)

- `chemx team check`: a coalesced or joined enqueue now revives a dead drain worker, and a failed spawn is printed and logged instead of swallowed. The tree fingerprint resolves paths against the repo toplevel, parses renames with `-z`, and covers dirty submodules (a shallow one on the enqueue path, about 80 to 125 ms in the elysium root on a busy tree, about 30 to 45 ms in the kit; a deep one only to verify a cache or join candidate and in the worker). A requester with no stable identity is refused. Cancelling a running check aborts its process tree and DMs the other requesters. Delivery is exception-safe and crash-safe (per-requester try/catch, one board post, a sweep of un-notified results at worker start and before idle exit), heartbeat errors no longer kill the worker, and workspace and test-failure reports keep their faults and details.
